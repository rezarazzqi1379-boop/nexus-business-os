## Core correction
Do not optimize for contact volume. Model:
COMPANY → SITE → PROCESS/MACHINE → CONSUMABLE/SPARE → SPEC/FAMILY → REPLACEMENT/PROCUREMENT TRIGGER → CURRENT DEMAND → PROCUREMENT ROUTE → INCUMBENT/RELATIONSHIP → TECHNICAL GATEKEEPER → DECISION ROLE → VERIFIED CONTACT → COMPLIANCE → SALES MOTION.

## Segmentation by buying mechanism
1. PORTAL-DRIVEN ENTERPRISE — supplier registration/qualification first; monitor plan/RFQ/RFI/tender.
2. TECHNICAL-QUALIFICATION BUYER — map plant/process, maintenance/engineering gatekeeper, approved equivalent/specification.
3. RELATIONSHIP-DRIVEN BUYER — verify incumbent/distributor/EPC/OEM/industry path before contact.
4. OPEN TENDER BUYER — bind exact item/spec/deadline/eligibility; historical tenders become learning fixtures.
5. EARLY-CAPEX BUYER — construction/commissioning/expansion creates hypothesis; identify equipment package/EPC/OEM before assuming consumable demand.

## Sales play
DISCOVER → VERIFY PROCESS → BIND PRODUCT/SPEC → VERIFY CURRENT TRIGGER → MAP PROCUREMENT METHOD → QUALIFICATION GAP → RELATIONSHIP PATH → TECHNICAL PROOF → COMMERCIAL FIT → VERIFIED CONTACT → COMPLIANCE → EVIDENCE_READY → HUMAN-GATED OUTREACH.

## Offer construction
Do not lead with generic company introduction. Lead with the buyer's machine/process/spec and reduce one measurable procurement risk: equivalence, lifetime, availability, lead time, documentation, trial quantity, logistics or total replacement cost. Never claim equivalence without technical evidence.

## Data to retain per customer
project_id; legal entity/site; country; industry; process/machine; product family/spec; observed demand event; event status/deadline; procurement portal; supplier registration requirements; incumbent; technical gatekeeper; decision role; relationship path; contact evidence; source origins; freshness; contradictions; compliance; next verification; sales motion; conversion stage.

## Current priority
For chain sales, mining/metallurgy/cement/material-handling/agriculture machinery are high-value discovery segments because chain/conveyor/spare procurement is observable. Priority is not a generic sector score; promotion requires product/process evidence and a current trigger.
