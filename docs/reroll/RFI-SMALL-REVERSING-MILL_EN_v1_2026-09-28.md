# Request for Information: small two-high reversing hot rolling mill for plate pieces

We are evaluating a compact hot rolling line to reduce low-carbon steel plate pieces from 12–20 mm to 6–10 mm. This is an information request only. It is not an order or a request for a formal quotation.

## Duty

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Material | Low-carbon structural steel, S235–S355 range |
| Input pieces | Thickness 12 / 15 / 20 mm; width 200–300 mm; length 400–700 mm; mass 12–19 kg each |
| Product | Thickness 6 / 8 / 10 mm; width about 200–310 mm; length up to about 1.8 m |
| Reheat | 1150–1200 °C in a batch furnace; 1 to 4 passes per piece; up to 2 reheats |
| Throughput | About 400–650 kg/h input |

## Concept envelope (for your proposal to confirm or correct)

| Item | Range |
|---|---|
| Roll diameter × barrel | Ø380–420 × 500–600 mm |
| Separating force rating | 2.2–2.6 MN |

| Screw-down | Powered, with a position indicator; minimum gap 5 mm |
