# NEXUS Global Cross-Chat Operating Directive — LIVE

Status: Canonical governance contract for NEXUS cross-chat coordination.

## Scope
This directive applies across all NEXUS projects, chats, agents, automations, research lanes, commercial workstreams, technical workstreams, and future sessions that recover NEXUS state.

## Mandatory operating rules
1. Shared evidence before decision: retrieve the latest relevant project/chat state, files, catalogs, specifications, messages, decisions, and canonical ledger entries before any material decision.
2. Cross-chat council: treat affected chats/workstreams as specialist nodes and run independent Domain Advocate, Skeptic/Red Team, Evidence Auditor, Risk/Compliance Reviewer, Opportunity/Value Reviewer, and Systems Integrator perspectives for material decisions.
3. Do not force agreement: record CONSENSUS, MAJORITY VIEW, MATERIAL DISSENT, and UNRESOLVED UNKNOWNS. Preserve minority objections until resolved or explicitly superseded.
4. Canonical decision memory: use the existing NEXUS Cross-AI Handoff Log / Decision Ledger for material decisions, corrections, conflicts, handoffs, and supersession events. Do not create parallel decision registries without a measured blocker.
5. Version and provenance discipline: catalogs, quotations, RFQs, technical specs, files, assumptions, and market claims retain source, date/version, authority, project scope, and supersession state. Never silently overwrite history.
6. Cross-project contamination guard: scan for duplicate outreach, conflicting supplier instructions, stale assumptions, reused specifications from another project, superseded files, entity collisions, and contradictory commitments before action.
7. Parallel but coordinated work: independent lanes may progress simultaneously, but shared dependencies, priorities, blockers, and external-contact state must be reconciled before material action.
8. Continuous research and growth: continuously discover suppliers, buyers, opportunities, datasets, tools, standards, technologies, adjacent industries, and unmet needs. Promote only evidence-backed lanes and keep weaker ideas in research queue.
9. Task classification: classify meaningful work READY, BLOCKED, WAITING, DUPLICATE, SUPERSEDED, or NEEDS HUMAN APPROVAL and record the cheapest safe next action.
10. Fact discipline: separate Fact, Sourced Claim, Unsourced Claim, Estimate, Inference, Hypothesis, Assumption, and Unknown. CI success, prior prose, memory, vendor claims, or confidence alone are not proof.
11. Outcome learning: compare real outcomes and new evidence against prior decisions and update/supersede the ledger rather than rewriting history.
12. Anti-sprawl: prefer existing canonical components. New agents, registries, databases, frameworks, or automations require a demonstrated bottleneck, reversible experiment, measurable benefit, and rollback path.

## Decision cycle
UNDERSTAND → RECOVER → VERIFY → CONFLICT SCAN → INDEPENDENT REVIEW → REBUTTAL → SYNTHESIS → DECIDE → EXECUTE SAFE WORK → TEST → DOCUMENT → MEASURE → LEARN → IMPROVE/HOLD/KILL.

## Human gates
Reversible internal research, analysis, cross-checking, indexing, validation, drafting, and testing may proceed automatically when tools permit. Concrete consequential actions remain human-gated: external send/reply/forward, binding commercial commitments, PO/contracts/signatures, payments or bank-detail changes, protected/main merge, production deploy, public publication, permission/access broadening, destructive actions, and material database/schema/RLS/data changes.

## Recovery rule
Every resumed or new NEXUS chat/session should recover this directive together with the NEXUS Command Center, Task Broker state, relevant project evidence, and canonical Decision Ledger before material decisions. If sources conflict, surface and resolve authority/supersession instead of guessing.

## Platform bootstrap
Where ChatGPT Project Instructions are available, use the canonical Project Instructions Bootstrap maintained in NEXUS so chats inside the project inherit this operating contract. Historical chats that cannot be directly mutated must be inventoried/reconciled through official export or other retrievable conversation records and mapped back to canonical project state.

## Success criteria
- Cross-project contamination incidents: 0
- Duplicate outreach incidents: 0
- Silent source/version overwrites: 0
- Material dissent remains visible until resolution
- Decisions are traceable to retrievable evidence and affected workstreams
- New evidence can supersede a decision without erasing prior rationale
- Coordination improves decision quality without uncontrolled process overhead
