## Buyer-model upgrade
A buyer record is not promoted by company size. Required sequence:
LEGAL ENTITY → SITE → PROCESS/MACHINE → EXACT PRODUCT FAMILY/SPEC → DATED DEMAND/TRIGGER → STATUS → PROCUREMENT ROUTE → ELIGIBILITY → INCUMBENT/AWARD → TECHNICAL GATEKEEPER → RELATIONSHIP PATH → VERIFIED CONTACT → COMPLIANCE → SALES MOTION.

## Offer-design rule
Generic introduction loses to a process-bound offer. Build the offer around one verified buyer problem: replacement compatibility, wear life, downtime, equivalent drawing/spec, trial quantity, delivery lead time, documentation/certification, logistics or total replacement cost. Never invent performance or equivalence.
