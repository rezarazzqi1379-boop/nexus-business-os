## Scope

Signals: 40ХН2МА, 38ХН3МФА, 42CrMo4. No cross-grade equivalence assumption.

## Strategies
A COMPANY_LIST — discover relevant companies; procurement only if naturally surfaced.
B CHAIN_FIRST — producer/importer/stockist/processor/end-user missing-node discovery.
C ADAPTIVE_PROCUREMENT_FIRST — begin with named high-information procurement demand.

## Equal budget
Exactly 3 public-web queries per strategy (9 total). Same evidence taxonomy and acceptance rules. Search result pages are observations; only evidence actually exposed by a source may be credited.

## Evidence classes
OFFICIAL_PROCUREMENT; OFFICIAL_COMPANY; OFFICIAL_GOVERNMENT_CUSTOMS; INDEPENDENT_SOURCE; COMMERCIAL_AGGREGATOR; SEARCH_SNIPPET. AI_INFERENCE has zero external-evidence weight.

## Acceptance
Qualified demand requires named buyer + product/grade/form demand supported by source evidence.
Verified relationship requires relationship-specific evidence under existing NEXUS evidence controls; tender+catalogue is insufficient.
Technical-fit opportunity cannot be EXACT_FIT with unknown dimensions/standard/delivery condition or unsupported grade equivalence.
Mirrors/copies share one source family.

## Metrics
qualified demand/search; named buyers/search; verified relationships/search; false edges prevented/search; technical-fit opportunities/search; information gain/search; resolution yield; source-quality distribution. Raw result count is not success.

## Interpretation
A_WINS, B_WINS, C_WINS, MIXED, or INCONCLUSIVE. Small sample alone cannot justify general superiority.
