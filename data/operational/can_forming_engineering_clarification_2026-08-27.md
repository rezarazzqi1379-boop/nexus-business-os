## Engineer-confirmation checklist before supplier reply
Please obtain/confirm the following buyer-side facts. Items intentionally remain UNKNOWN until engineering confirms them:

1. Exact finished can geometry for the selected SKU: body OD, body height, neck OD before/after forming, flange dimensions and bead geometry/locations.
2. Mandatory operations for this purchase stage: necking only; necking + flanging; necking + flanging + beading; or combination including seaming.
3. Number of necking/forming stages and whether 8 beads is genuinely required.
4. Required guaranteed continuous production rate, not supplier maximum nameplate speed.
5. Machine installation height from finished floor level.
6. Can centerline / conveyor elevation at machine inlet and outlet.
7. Number of inlet paths and outlet paths; orientation and flow direction.
8. Interface dimensions and synchronization signals with the existing upstream/downstream production equipment.
9. Minimum/maximum accumulation buffer required before and after the machine.
10. Available footprint and maintenance/service clearance.
11. Electrical supply, installed-power limit, compressed-air pressure/flow and any cooling/lubrication utilities available at site.
12. Tinplate material/temper/thickness range to be processed.
13. Required tooling/change parts and acceptable changeover time.
14. Required guarding, interlocks, emergency-stop integration and line-safety interface.
15. Required acceptance test: target SKU, continuous run duration, accepted reject/jam rate, guaranteed CPM, dimensional tolerances and repeatability.
16. Required spare-parts package, commissioning/training scope and documentation/PLC backup requirements.
