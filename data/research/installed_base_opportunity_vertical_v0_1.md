# Installed-Base Opportunity Vertical v0.1

Status: INTERNAL RESEARCH CONTRACT. No outreach authority.

## Purpose
Turn public/owned evidence about factories, equipment, lines, maintenance, CAPEX, tenders, shutdowns and hiring into reviewable retrofit/spares/replacement opportunities without inventing demand.

## Required evidence packet
Each candidate must bind:
- company/facility stable identifier;
- project/category scope;
- source locator(s) and retrieval date;
- observed installed-base or operational signal;
- epistemic class for each signal;
- temporal status (current / historical / superseded / conflicted);
- explicit unmet-need hypothesis separated from facts;
- reversible validation step;
- acceptance test;
- rollback / reject condition.

## Signals allowed to raise a candidate
FACT/MEASUREMENT-backed examples: installed machine/line, published tender, documented maintenance shutdown, disclosed expansion/CAPEX, hiring for a specific production technology, verified spare-part requirement.
CLAIM-only examples may create WATCH/VALIDATE candidates but cannot create a sales-ready lead.

## Hard gates
- no cross-project specification transfer;
- no duplicate company/facility/opportunity IDs;
- stale operational signals require refresh before consequential use;
- inferred need is HYPOTHESIS until independently validated;
- no contact discovery or outreach is authorized by scoring alone;
- KCl/Can Forming consequential candidates require live refresh per portfolio gate;
- Heat Treatment remains paused unless management status changes;
- Hydrotester follow-up remains duplicate-blocked unless new evidence creates a materially new action.

## First acceptance proof
Use a synthetic or already-held evidence packet to produce at least three candidates:
1. one EXPERIMENT/VALIDATE candidate with strong evidence and reversible next step;
2. one WATCH candidate with incomplete evidence;
3. one REJECT candidate due to cross-project contamination, critical risk or unsupported need.

Pass only if every decision is explainable from evidence refs, temporal state and the portfolio gate, with zero external action.
