Fit rule used in this file (stated so the scores can be challenged):
- Strong = 1000-2500 kW AND base speed stated at about 500-650 rpm AND location in a target country.
- Partial = kW in 900-2800 but base speed missing or at the 650-750 edge, OR spec matches but the unit is outside the target countries, OR spec matches but the page could not be read.
- Weak = spec out of band, contradictory data, not verifiably DC, or sold only inside a whole line.
Fit is separate from freshness. Freshness is shown in its own column.

## 4. Quote-request checklist (for each Strong / Partial unit, after owner approval)

Ask every seller the following, in writing, and ask for photos and documents rather than statements:

1. Nameplate photo (full, readable): maker, model, serial, year, rated kW, armature V and A, field V and A, base and max rpm, duty class (S1?), insulation class, IP / IC cooling, weight.
2. Test report: last no-load and load run, with date and place. Ask if a full-speed run under load is possible before purchase.
3. Insulation resistance and polarization index for armature, field, interpole/compensation windings, with megger voltage and date.
4. Commutator condition: photos, runout, bar-to-bar resistance, undercut depth, remaining copper height, any turning or re-skimming history.
5. Brushes and brush gear: grade, remaining length, springs, holders, spares included.
6. Bearings: type (sleeve or rolling), last replacement date, shaft-current insulation, temperature sensors.
7. Winding history: original or rewound, by whom and when, treatment (VPI), any burnt or repaired coils.
8. Spares in the lot: brush sets, coils, commutator, bearings, terminal box, heater.
9. Ancillaries: blower motor and duct, air-water cooler (for IC W37A86), tacho or encoder, thermistors / Pt100, heaters. Which are included.
10. Field and armature voltage compatibility with the mill drive (660 V or 750 V thyristor stack) and any derating.
11. Price: delivered price and EXW / FOB with loading, currency, validity date, VAT / export invoice status, payment terms, inspection right before payment, warranty.
12. Whether the seller owns the unit or is an agent, and the reason for sale.

Unit-specific asks: S1 whether the motor is the original dragline hoist type and its mounting and shaft; P6 speed range and which of the three city postings holds the unit; P5 whether Kemsan sells stock or builds to order; P1/P2/P3/P4 armature voltage 500-750 V versus the mill supply, plus export licence and freight from the US.
