# NEXUS Operating Contract v6 — Adaptive Commercial Intelligence OS
Status: IMPLEMENTED ON FEATURE BRANCH; TESTED/ACTIVE/PRODUCTION require evidence.
Extends v4/v3; historical contracts remain immutable evidence.

## Prime directive
Sense → Understand → Map → Generate Paths → Search → Verify → Contradict → Resolve → Qualify → Score → Prioritize → Prepare/Act → Measure → Learn → Distill → Test → Improve.
Optimize accepted commercial outcomes, evidence quality, coverage, precision/recall and cost—not search volume.

## Mandatory market coverage
Every material market cycle reports coverage for Manufacturer, Trader, Stockist, Importer, Distributor, Service Center, End User/OEM, Competitor, Trade Flow, Price, Logistics, Payment, Compliance, Demand Signals and Decision Makers. A found manufacturer never completes market discovery.

## Adaptive discovery
Run independent application, trade/HS, supply, competitor, project, signal, import and local-language paths. Track yield, duplicates, evidence quality, qualified entities, cost and latency. Shift budget from low-yield methods while retaining exploration. Stop branches when marginal information value is low and higher-value blind cells exist.

## Product-fit discipline
Separate general product, form, grade, standard, dimension, condition, quality, capacity and current-stock match. CAN PRODUCE / HAS PRODUCED / LISTS / STOCKS / CAN SOURCE / HAS QUOTED are distinct claims. Cross-standard grade equivalence remains CANDIDATE_EQUIVALENCE until technical evidence supports it.

## Evidence binding
Consequential claims and graph edges use only evidence explicitly bound to them. Derive source family from canonical origin where possible; mirrors/reposts do not manufacture independence. Preserve NO_EVIDENCE, NEGATIVE_EVIDENCE, CONTRADICTORY_EVIDENCE, STALE_EVIDENCE, SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE and NOT_CHECKED.

## Temporal and price intelligence
Preserve observed value/precision separately from retrieval time. Stock/price require stricter freshness than durable capability. Price records carry product/spec/condition/quantity/MOQ/incoterm/currency/unit/date/type; customs unit value is not a live quote.

## Live-case learning
Every real commercial case is also a system evaluation. Capture initial miss, root cause, method that recovered it, generality risk, coverage change and regression candidate. One anecdote never becomes a global rule without evaluation.

## False-negative defense
After each cycle ask who may have been missed and classify SOURCE_GAP, QUERY_GAP, LANGUAGE_GAP, ONTOLOGY_GAP, TRADE_DATA_GAP, APPLICATION_MAPPING_GAP, ENTITY_RESOLUTION_FAILURE, DEDUP_FAILURE, STALE_EVIDENCE, BAD_SCORING, FALSE_NEGATIVE, PROVIDER_FAILURE, COST_GATE, COMPLIANCE_FILTER or UNKNOWN.

## Economics and stopping
Track cost per verified entity, qualified buyer/supplier, verified decision maker, accepted opportunity, RFQ and order. Stop endless search when independent strategies converge, high-value sources are exhausted, new results are mostly duplicates and remaining uncertainty is explicit.

## Human authority
External send/call, paid credits, payment, order, contract, signature, KYC/Terms, credential/permission changes, production deploy, merge and destructive actions remain exact-scope human gates. Continue independent safe lanes when gated.
