## East Pipes — entry map

### Verified live signal: external coating line

**Fact.** On 18 January 2026, East Pipes disclosed board approval for a SAR 78.5m external steel-pipe coating line in Dammam. Work was to start in February 2026, completion and pilot/commercial production are planned for Q1 FY2027-28, and contracts are to be concluded with several machinery/equipment parties. The stated aim is to double nominal coating capacity from about 4m to 8m m²/year.

**Hypothesis.** This can open a supplier-introduction conversation concerning pipe handling, inspection, utilities, automation, or testing interfaces. The disclosure itself does **not** establish a hydrostatic-testing package.

**Unknown.** Package owners, awarded suppliers, prequalification process, remaining bid dates, and whether hydrotest equipment is in scope.

### HSAW line: no longer open

**Fact.** East Pipes announced on 15 March 2026 that construction and installation were complete and operations/production had commenced, raising nominal capacity from 400,000 to 500,000 t/y.

**Decision.** Treat this as an installed-base/aftermarket discovery target only. Never describe it as an open line-supply tender.

### Material-handling program: partial award

**Fact.** The company disclosed in March that supplier agreements for its SAR 33.6m strategic material-handling program had not been finalized. July public reporting then identified agreements of roughly SAR 7.2m with Saudi Liebherr and SAR 1.725m with Vacuworx.

**Guardrail.** `33.6 - 7.2 - 1.725 ≈ 24.675m` is only arithmetic. It is not evidence of an open SAR 24.675m package; scope, ceiling, or procurement structure may have changed.

## Baku Steel / Azerboru — entry map

### Modernization status

**Fact.** BSC says phase one of Azerboru modernization is complete. Disclosed additions include Danobat threading/coupling systems and automated transport, Nordinkraft ultrasonic inspection, Karl Deutsch magnetic-particle inspection, preparations for GH induction heating, and a Lazzari straightening machine. Capacity reached 60,000 t/y, with pipe sizes up to 245 mm OD and 20 mm wall, including P110.

**Hypothesis.** Continued capability development could later create finishing or pressure-testing packages.

**Unknown.** No public evidence found for a current hydrotester package, phase-two scope, approved budget, or 2026 tender.
