## Technical requirements

- Reversing-mill duty (frequent direction reversal during rolling)
- Requested in your response:
  - Model
  - Continuous power AND short-time overload power (please state separately)
  - **The ambient temperature, altitude, and duty/service class (e.g. S1–S6 per IEC 60034-1) on which your stated continuous power rating is based** — please do not quote a bare continuous-power figure without stating these conditions, since continuous rating is not meaningful without them

  - Rated reversals per hour
  - IP rating and cooling method
  - Encoder/tacho type
  - Moment of inertia (GD²/J)
  - Insulation class
  - Weight
- Please quote the motor, the four-quadrant converter/drive, transformer, reactor, and power/control panels as SEPARATE line items, not bundled into one figure
