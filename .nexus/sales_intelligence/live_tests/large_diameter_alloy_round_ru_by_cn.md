# Live Discovery Test — Large-Diameter Alloy Steel Round Bar

Observed: 2026-10-05
Scope: Russia / Belarus / China; alloy/special steel round bar, target diameter 250–600 mm.
Status: RESEARCH EVIDENCE — not RFQ, quote, order, or transaction approval.

## Verified / supported findings
- Urals Stampings Plant (Mechel): official Mechel catalogue supports forged round bars D 90–1500 mm, L up to 7000 mm, mass up to 45 t; listed grades include C15E/R, C45E/R, 16MnCr5, 18CrNiMo7-6, 25CrMo4, 34CrNiMo6 and 42CrMo4. Strong dimensional/product fit for 250–600 mm.
- OEMK / Metalloinvest: official product page supports SBQ round bars only through D 180 mm. Reject as a direct 250–600 mm match unless separate forging evidence is found.
- Belarus large-diameter domestic production: NOT VERIFIED in this cycle. Do not infer capability from general steelmaking.
- Belarus import evidence: 2024 mirror/export data for HS 722830 shows China 80,630 kg / USD 159.62k; Turkey 43,140 kg / USD 44.22k; Poland 6,929 kg / USD 24.59k. A shipment-level commercial dataset also shows China→Belarus alloy bars including diameters 250, 275, 280, 300, 350 and 420 mm, 24,718 kg / USD 55,913.149.
- Russia import evidence: 2024 mirror/export data for HS 722830 shows China 1,332,190 kg / USD 3,174.87k; Turkey 120,412 kg / USD 264.57k; Uzbekistan 56,000 kg / USD 34.17k; Kazakhstan 233 kg / USD 10.05k; India 1,000 kg / USD 2.13k.
- China supplier candidate: Jiangsu Zhuhong marketplace evidence supports forged round bar 90–800 mm, including 42CrMo4, C45/CK45, 16MnCr5, 20MnCr5 and other grades; MOQ claim 15 MT/size and 6000 t/month are supplier/marketplace claims, not independently verified capacity.

## Red-team conclusions
- HS 722830 is a trade-flow proxy, not proof that every shipment is forged or in the target diameter. Forged material may fall under 722840 depending on condition/classification.
- Trade unit values are customs averages, not current executable quotations.
- Grade equivalence is not accepted without standard/MTC/chemistry/mechanical-property evidence.
- Russia/Belarus commercial attractiveness remains separate from compliance and transaction readiness.
- Supplier marketplace claims require direct document verification before Tier-A supplier promotion.
