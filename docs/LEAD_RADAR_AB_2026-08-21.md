## Verified signals

### 1. Baku Steel / Azerboru — Azerbaijan

Fact: phase-one modernization doubled capacity to 60,000 tonnes, expanded production up to 245 mm diameter and 20 mm wall, added P110, threading, automated handling, ultrasonic inspection and magnetic-particle inspection. The company says it will continue developing production capability.

Hypothesis: future modernization phases may need additional finishing, heat-treatment, hydrotest, handling or inspection systems.

Unknown: exact next-phase scope, budget, procurement timing and commercial feasibility with an Iranian counterparty.

Disposition: priority research; do not contact yet.

### 2. East Pipes — Saudi Arabia

Fact: a new HSAW line adds about 100,000 t/y to nominal 400,000 t/y capacity. A separate external coating-line project is planned to double coating capacity from about four to eight million square metres per year. The company states that several machinery/equipment contracts are or will be involved.

Hypothesis: expansion can create downstream testing, material-handling, coating and quality-control requirements.

Unknown: which packages are already awarded, whether hydrotest/finishing equipment is included, vendor-registration route and sanctions/payment feasibility.

Disposition: priority research; procurement-package mapping before any approach.

### 3. Tashkent Pipe Plant — Uzbekistan

Fact: EBRD announced up to US$20 million financing for insulated large-diameter pipe production, raw material and coating-equipment upgrades.

Hypothesis: adjacent testing or handling modernization may exist.

Counterevidence: the verified financing is specifically tied to insulation/coating, not OCTG heat treatment or a 120 MPa hydrotester.

Disposition: research, lower priority than Baku Steel and East Pipes.

### 4. TBX Nexxia — UAE

Fact: Mubadala and Tubacex launched a fully operational 20,000 t/y CRA OCTG platform in Abu Dhabi, backed by an earlier US$200 million investment and ADNOC as cornerstone client.

Hypothesis: optimization, maintenance or later expansion could generate equipment needs.

Counterevidence: the platform is already operational; no open machinery package is proven.

Disposition: relationship and future-signal monitoring, not immediate equipment outreach.

### 5. QazExpoCentre-Pipe — Kazakhstan

Fact: a new threading workshop launched in Uralsk on 21 April 2026.

Hypothesis: further localization stages may extend into testing, inspection or heat treatment.

Unknown: current testing capacity and next approved investment stage.

Disposition: research and monitor.

### 6. Vallourec / Sonatrach award

Fact: Vallourec announced an OCTG supply award from Sonatrach.

Classification: supply-side market signal; not evidence that Vallourec is an equipment-buyer lead for ATF.

Disposition: supply/market intelligence only.
